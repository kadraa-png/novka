# Privacy policy

Novka keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no server, and no
analytics.

The only thing Novka downloads is a list of exchange rates. Nothing about you or
your money is sent with that request.

With notification access, Novka reads payment notifications from Google Wallet and
Raiffeisen, and SMS notifications from Raiffeisen. Other notifications are ignored
and never stored. One-time codes in bank messages are replaced with dots before the
message is saved.

Backups and CSV files are written only where you choose to save them. A backup
without a password is not encrypted.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

The same text is shown in the app under More, Privacy. Questions can go to the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/kadraa-png/novka/issues).
