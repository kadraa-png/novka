# Changelog

## 1.0 (2026-11-13)

First release.

- Accounts in any currency, with transfers and cached exchange rates.
- Automatic capture of payments from Google Wallet and Raiffeisen (BiH)
  notifications, with duplicate merging.
- Budgets, recurring transactions, savings goals and debts.
- Insights graphs.
- Backup and restore with an optional password; CSV export and import.
- App lock with PIN and fingerprint.
- Balance widget.
- Material You design, light and dark themes.
- English and Bosnian interface.
- About links to GitHub, the website and Ko-fi.
